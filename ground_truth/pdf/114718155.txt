Correcting display resolution:
- On the grub screen, press to enter the command line
- Enter or to check available resolutions
- Open , and edit to match your resolution
- Finally, run to update your grub config
Setting a custom background:
- Make sure you have installed, or at least something that provides
- Find the resolution of your display, and make sure your background matches the resolution
- 1920x1080 1080p
- 2560x1080 ultrawide
- 2560x1440 2k
- 3440x1440 ultrawide2k
- 3840x2160 4k
- Place your custom background inside the root of the project, and name it
- Run the installer like normal, but with -s and -t and -i
- Make sure to replace with your resolution and with the theme
