ORDINANCE NO. 2197

AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF SUGAR LAND, TEXAS,
PROVIDING FOR A CHANGE OF ZONING FROM BUSINESS OFFICE (B-O)
DISTRICT TO PLANNED DEVELOPMENT (PD) DISTRICT FINAL DEVELOPMENT
PLAN FOR APPROXIMATELY 0.7906 ACRES OF LAND LOCATED AT THE
SOUTHEAST CORNER OF LAKE POINTE PARKWAY AND CREEK BEND DRIVE.

WHEREAS, the current property owner has requested that approximately 0.7906 acres of
land located within the City of Sugar Land (the "City"), at the Southeast corner of Lake Pointe
Parkway and Creek Bend Drive, be rezoned from Business Office (B-O) District to Planned
Development (PD) District Final Development Plan; and

WHEREAS, the City Planning and Zoning Commission forwarded its final report to the
City Council, recommending approval of the rezoning request; and

WHEREAS, the City Planning and Zoning Commission and the City Council have each
conducted, in the time and manner and after notice required by law and applicable ordinances, a
public hearing on such requested zoning change; and

WHEREAS, the City Planning and Zoning Commission recommended granting such
requested zoning change and the same is herein incorporated and made a part of this ordinance;
and

WHEREAS, the City Council finds that the zoning request complies with the City's
comprehensive plan and now deems it appropriate to make such zoning change; NOW,
THEREFORE:

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL
OF THE CITY OF SUGAR LAND, TEXAS:

Section 1. That the facts and recitations set forth in the preamble of the ordinance are
hereby declared true and correct.

Section 2. That the zoning district classification of approximately 0.7906 acres of land
located at the southeast corner of Lake Pointe Parkway and Creek Bend Drive, described in Exhibit
A attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference, is changed from Business Office (B-O)
zoning district classification to Planned Development (PD) District Final Development Plan
zoning district classification under the comprehensive zoning ordinance of the City of Sugar Land,
Texas.

Section 3. That the City's official zoning map be amended to reflect this change in zoning
district classification.

Section 4. That the following Exhibits are attached hereto and incorporated into this
ordinance:

B. General Provisions, Definitions, and Exhibits.

1. General Provisions. In accordance with Section 2-133, the PD must be
constructed, developed, and maintained in accordance with this final development
plan.

2. Definitions. Except as otherwise provided for in this final development plan, the
words used in this plan have the meaning established by the Development Code.
In this final development plan:

Development Code means the Sugar Land Development Code, as amended.

Live/Work Townhome means a Townhome in which up to 550 square feet of only
the ground floor is devoted to a non-residential use as permitted by this ordinance.

Open Space means the portion of all land contained within the PD that is not
covered by buildings, parking lots, driveways, or other impermeable material.

PD means the planned development district created by this ordinance.

Townhome means a single-family attached dwelling.

Shared/Common Driveway means a privately-owned and maintained vehicular
accessway that provides access from a public street to townhome lots.

D. Development Regulations for Townhome Lots. As shown on Exhibit B-2 (Townhome
Lot Configuration), townhome sites within the PD shall be developed in accordance with
the following regulations:

1. Land Use: Live/Work Townhomes

2. Building finishes: Siding, masonry, stucco, metal roof, metal/glass railing,
aluminum windows

3. Maximum number of lots: 9

4. Minimum lot area: 2,842 sf

5. Minimum lot width: 28'

6. Minimum lot depth: 101.5'

7. Maximum lot coverage:

Building footprint 1,115 sf
Sidewalk/courtyard 440 sf
Rear private drive 590 sf
Total Coverage (75.5%) 2,145 sf

8. Maximum height of structures: 50'

2

9. Minimum building setbacks:

(a) Front setback: 25'
(b) Side setback: 0'/6' - Zero foot (0') on one side with six feet (6') on adjacent
side
(c) Street side setback: Lot 1 - 15'
Lot 9 - 40'
(d) Rear setback: 18' from the cantilever on the 2nd floor
(e) Garage setback: 21'

10. Parking Layout: Two (2) private garage parking spaces provided per unit.
Residents will have an option to add vehicle lifts within the garage to accommodate
up to four (4) vehicles within the garage. Six (6) additional on-site visitor/guest
parking spaces will be provided behind the gates. Pedestrian access to these spaces
shall be provided by a gate leading into the rear courtyard for each townhome. Two
(2) additional on-street parallel parking spaces will be added to the existing seven
(7) on-street spaces on Lake Pointe Parkway. Additionally, three (3) additional
parallel parking spaces will be added to the exit side of the private access drive at
the south side shown as Reserve A on Exhibit B-1 (Site Plan).

E. Shared Common Driveway.

The minimum pavement width of the shared common driveway must be eighteen feet (18')
and shall be concrete.

F. Parking Regulations.

1. Parking spaces must be provided for Townhomes as required by Chapter 2, Article
II, Section 2-71 of the Development Code, which is the primary use.

2. Live/Work Townhomes must comply with the parking requirements applicable to
the primary use of Townhomes as stated in item 1 above and also provide one (1)
space for each 550 square feet of non-residential use. Two (2) additional on-street
parking spaces will be constructed as shown on Exhibit B-1 (Site Plan) and those
two (2) spaces will be credited toward the Live/Work Townhome parking
requirements. No additional on-street parking on Lake Pointe Parkway will be
credited toward meeting the parking requirements.

3. The Townhomes shall be deed restricted to two (2) vehicles (or four [4] vehicles
with the addition of a vehicle lift in the garage] per unit that must be parked in the
garage. All exterior surface parking (including street parking) shall be used for
short-term guest or employee parking, and residents will be prohibited from parking
in any of the exterior surface spaces.

3

G. Landscape Regulations. As shown on Exhibit B-5 (Landscape, Open Space & Pedestrian
Plan), the PD shall be developed in accordance with the following landscape regulations:

1. Landscape Buffers:

(a) A ten foot (10')-wide minimum buffer, contiguous to the lot line, shall be
provided along the side of the public street adjacent to the townhome lots.

(b) Ten foot (10')-wide minimum buffers, contiguous to lot lines, shall be
provided along Lake Pointe Parkway and Creek Bend Drive.

(c) Required buffers may include the pedestrian walkway, steps, and landing
connecting the public street sidewalk to the front door of a townhome.

2. Open Space:

Required minimum open space of twenty percent (20%) has been provided along
Lake Pointe Parkway and Creek Bend Drive.

3. Shade trees:

(a) Trees required per Section 2-384 (Residential Front Yard Landscaping
Requirements) of the Development Code may be located within public
street right-of-way, landscape buffers, or the front or side yard of any lot
provided that one (1) tree is within fifty feet (50') of every lot. If located in
a side yard, the tree shall be less than thirty feet (30') from a public street
right-of-way.

(b) Approximate distribution and location of shade trees within Reserve A and
Reserve B is shown on Exhibit B-5 (Landscape, Open Space & Pedestrian
Plan).

(c) Shade trees shall have a minimum four inch (4") caliper and minimum ten
foot (10') height as measured at ground level when planted.

(d) Required shade trees shall have a minimum spacing of twenty feet (20').

4. Plant list:

Plant species used to satisfy the requirements of the PD are listed on Exhibit B-7
(Plant List).

4

H. Pedestrian Circulation Regulations. As shown on Exhibit B-5 (Landscape, Open Space
& Pedestrian Plan), the PD shall be developed in accordance with the following pedestrian
circulation regulations:

1. A six-foot (6') pedestrian sidewalk towards Creek Bend Drive and a six-foot (6')
pedestrian sidewalk on the south side of Creek Bend Drive.

2. All sidewalks required herein shall be paved with concrete.

3. Lighting used to illuminate sidewalks shall be arranged, located, or screened to
direct light away from residential lots.

I. Live/Work Townhome Sign Regulations. The following sign regulations shall apply to
the PD:

1. Freestanding signs are prohibited.

2. Wall signs must not:

(a) Exceed three square feet (3') or have a dimension exceeding thirty-six
inches (36");

(b) Have internal or exterior lighting; or

(c) Be constructed of any material other than wood or metal.

3. Signs advertising the non-residential use must not be located in or affixed to any
glass of a window or door of the non-residential use so as to be visible from any
public street.

EXHIBIT B-3

ALLOWABLE NON-RESIDENTIAL
LAND USES IN LIVE/WORK TOWNHOMES
FOR CREEK BEND LIVE/WORK TOWNHOMES

Limited to Professional Office

Up to 550 square feet of only the ground floor may be devoted to a non-residential use as listed
above. The premises of the Live/Work Townhome must be used as the primary residence of the
property owner, which shall also be the same as the non-residential use owner/operator. The total
number of live/work townhomes shall not exceed nine (9). All garages must remain the primary
parking for the property owner and may not be utilized in the square footage of a non-residential
use on the ground floor. In addition, if a property owner decides to operate a non-residential use
as listed above in this exhibit, the business may not rely on more than one (1) employee located at
the property and shall conduct business by appointment only.
