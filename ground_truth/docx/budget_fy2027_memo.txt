Interoffice Memorandum

To: All Department Heads From: Priya Rao, Chief Financial Officer Date:
August 4, 2026 Subject: Fiscal Year 2027 Budget Submission Guidelines

Purpose

This memo sets out the process and deadlines for submitting departmental
budget requests for fiscal year 2027. Please read it in full before
preparing your submission.

Submission Requirements

Each department must submit a budget package containing the following:

1.  A one-page narrative summarizing priorities and expected outcomes.
2.  A line-item spreadsheet broken down by cost category.
3.  Headcount plans, including any requested new positions.
4.  Justification for any increase exceeding five percent over the prior
    year.

Key Dates

- September 8 — Draft budgets due to Finance for initial review.
- September 22 — Review meetings with the CFO office.
- October 6 — Final revised budgets due.
- October 20 — Executive committee approval.

Guiding Principles

Requests should reflect realistic demand rather than aspirational
targets. Where a program is being wound down, please redirect the
associated funds rather than assuming they carry forward. Contact the
Finance business partner assigned to your group with any questions.

Thank you for your cooperation in keeping this cycle on schedule.
