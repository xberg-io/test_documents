# Product Launch Plan — Aurora Mobile App

## Overview

Aurora is our new consumer budgeting application launching in the fourth
quarter. This plan captures the milestones, owners, and messaging needed
for a successful public release across web and mobile channels.

## Launch Phases

1.  **Private beta** — invite 500 existing customers for two weeks.
2.  **Open beta** — public waitlist with staged access.
3.  **General availability** — full launch with paid tiers enabled.

## Marketing Milestones

- Teaser campaign begins six weeks before launch.
- Press briefings held two weeks before general availability.
- Launch-day webinar and live demo for enterprise prospects.

## Success Metrics

| Metric              | Target   |
|---------------------|----------|
| Beta signups        | 10000    |
| Day-one activations | 4000     |
| 30-day retention    | 45%      |
| Support ticket rate | Below 3% |

## Risks

The primary risk is app-store review timing. To mitigate, we will submit
the production build ten days ahead of the planned launch date and keep
a fallback release window in reserve.
