Change management report

This report covers the review period and records how change requests were handled. It is written for readers who need the outcome rather than the detail, so the substance is in the table on the next page and in the figure that follows it.

Requests arrive from three sources: the on-call rota, the quarterly planning cycle, and direct customer escalations. All three enter the same queue and are ranked by impact before anyone is assigned to them.

The period saw fewer emergency changes than the one before it, which the team attributes to the longer soak time now required before a release is promoted.
